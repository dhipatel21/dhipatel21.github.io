01 · About

A bit about how I work

I'm a software engineer on the MX Routing team at Cisco Meraki, where I work on the networking stack that powers thousands of security appliances — from VPN NAT logic to ECMP and BGP-based dynamic routing. Lately I've been leading our team's AI initiative: architecting a multi-agent LLM workflow that takes a support ticket end-to-end to a merge request, using a graph-based approach to orchestrate planning, code generation, and review agents.

Before that, I built embedded firmware at Nordson, configuring STM32 microcontrollers, JTAG, and Ethernet PHYs, and got my start at Meraki as a firmware intern overhauling test infrastructure with Jenkins, Gerrit, and Yocto.

I graduated Summa Cum Laude from the University of Michigan with a B.S. in Computer Engineering, where my projects spanned computer vision, robotics, and embedded control systems.
